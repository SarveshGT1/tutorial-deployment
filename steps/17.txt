title: Sign in and find your agent
[do]
- Press Enter. The script runs  pac auth create : a browser window
  asks you to sign in with your GT account
- It shows which environment you're signed in to. Not the workshop
  environment? Answer n, and paste its URL from the list
- It runs  pac copilot list  and looks for **Barista {NAME}**
[why]
If pac can list your agent, your laptop can reach the environment
from code: your account, your licence and GT's sign-in rules all
said yes. Everything else in the CLI builds on this one check.

It's also the first thing to try on any new laptop or environment,
before you build anything on top of it.
[what]
pac auth create stores a sign-in profile for your Windows account.
Every pac command after that uses it until you sign in again.
   pac org who        which environment you're signed in to
   pac env list       every environment you can reach, with URLs
   pac auth create --environment <url>   switch to another one

An environment URL looks like  https://<org>.crm4.dynamics.com .
pac copilot list shows the same agents as the Agents page in
Copilot Studio, for one environment at a time.
[help]
AADSTS50078 in the error? Your sign-in expired. Run the step again
and finish the multi-factor prompt in the browser.
AADSTS530035? GT's sign-in rules block this device or app. Only IT
can change that; the rest of the workshop still works without pac.
Your agent isn't in the list? You're in another environment: answer
n and paste the workshop environment's URL.
