ACT 1 · THE DATA

Every agent starts as a folder. Before any AI, there is a place
where the data lives, and someone who decides what goes in it.

   Site ─── Library ─── Folder ─── File ─── Agent

In this act you make that folder and put an Excel table in it.
[do]

- Put your browser on the right half
- Press **w** for the why, **i** for what happens inside

[why]
About thirty people build in the same Copilot Studio environment
today. Your name goes into every flow and agent you make, so you
can find yours and nobody overwrites anybody else's.

Read the why and what screens. Each one gives you XP, and the quiz
after every step asks about them.
[what]
This script is plain Bash. It runs on your laptop and never talks
to Microsoft itself. It does four things:
- copies the workshop Excel file into your OneDrive
- opens the right web page at the right moment
- puts the agent instructions on your clipboard
- checks the numbers your flow and agent give back

Progress is saved in the file .progress next to this script.
Press q any time; run it again and you continue where you stopped.
[quiz]
Where will your agent read its data from?
a) From this laptop
b) From a SharePoint library: your OneDrive
c) From inside Copilot Studio
answer: b
because: The script only copies the file. The flow and the agent read it in the cloud, from your OneDrive, and OneDrive for Business is a SharePoint library.
