title: Make the folder, drop in the data
[do]
- Press Enter. The script makes the folder **{FOLDER}**
  in your OneDrive and copies **{FILE}** into it
- Watch the OneDrive cloud icon in the taskbar: it syncs in seconds
- If the script can't find OneDrive, it tells you how to upload
  the file by hand. That works just as well.
[why]
The folder you connect is where the agent is born. Whoever can open
the folder can see what the agent sees: permissions come from the
library, not from the agent.

That's why you choose the folder first. A tidy folder with one clear
file gives a reliable agent. A dump of everything gives an agent
that guesses.
[what]
OneDrive for Business is not a separate cloud. It is a SharePoint
document library on your personal site:
   gtnl-my.sharepoint.com/personal/<you>

The OneDrive app on your laptop syncs the folder:
   your laptop  ──>  OneDrive app  ──>  SharePoint library

The script finds the folder through the Windows variable
OneDriveCommercial. If that's empty it looks for "OneDrive - ...".
It never overwrites a koffie.xlsx that's already there.
[quiz]
OneDrive for Business is...
a) A separate cloud next to SharePoint
b) A SharePoint document library on your personal site
c) A backup of your laptop's C: drive
answer: b
because: Your OneDrive lives on gtnl-my.sharepoint.com: the same SharePoint, on a site that belongs to you.
[help]
"Can't find OneDrive": upload by hand. In the browser: OneDrive,
**+ Nieuw** (New), **Map** (Folder), name it {FOLDER}, open it,
**Uploaden** (Upload), **Bestanden** (Files), pick data/{FILE}.
"Permission denied" or "busy": koffie.xlsx is open in desktop Excel.
Close it and press b, then Enter, to retry.
Red cross on the OneDrive icon: sign in to OneDrive first.
